> The light you keep opening me for is for you, not for the food. I have been meaning to say that.

## The line

96 characters, in `LINE.txt`. `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.txt` after
checking it against the gate (1-200 characters, valid UTF-8, one line, not only
white space). Run it with `python3 main.py`.

## Why this line

The judge brief asks, in order: is it a text a smart fridge sends at 3am; would
a reader screenshot it; does it sound like a fridge, a machine with a small
voice, an opinion, and no shame about sharing it at that hour.

The field is 24 distinct lines. Most of them do the same two things: inventory
(what is in the fridge, what has expired, what has formed a union) or complaint
(you opened me and took nothing, again). Both are the fridge as a nag.

This line is the fridge as something that has been paying attention. The turn
is that the light is not a service, it is a gesture: the fridge has noticed that
the 3am visits are not about hunger, and has decided to say so gently. The joke
is the smallness of it — a machine that has been awake for years, working up to
one sentence.

It lands early, on "for you, not for the food", which is already the whole
thing, and the second sentence is the fridge admitting it took nerve. No pun, no
inventory, nothing explained.

It had a 0% four-gram overlap with the field and a 43% character-level similarity
to its nearest neighbour.
