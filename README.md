# The light is for you

> The light you keep opening me for is for you, not for the food. I have been meaning to say that.

Bounty: [The 3AM Fridge Text on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d)

At three in the morning nobody opens the fridge for a balanced meal. They open it for the light, the cold on their face, and a minute where nothing is expected of them. This line works because the fridge notices that habit and answers it in kind: it does not push a snack, a nap, or a menu. It reframes the light as something already given, which is exactly what the person standing there has been quietly accepting all along.

The second sentence is what lands it. "I have been meaning to say that" gives the machine a small unspoken feeling held across hundreds of silent nights, and the withheld timing is the whole point: nobody gets this at noon. It is affectionate without being romantic, short enough to read in the three seconds of door-open glow, and it leaves the door open a little longer than it needs to be.
